The brief named nine images but supplied descriptions only for #1–6.
No additional subjects have been invented.

Every generation and refinement used garden-desktop.png as its first
style reference and included the exact shared style/lighting/alpha line.
Generated using the built-in image-generation tool.

The sunflower is one generated sheet, not a composite of separately
generated plants. Slice at x=0, 1024, 2048 and 3072; each frame is
1024 x 2048. Frames run: two-leaf sprout, young leafy plant, closed green
bud, full bloom. The panel divisions are fully transparent. No individual
stage alignment or slicing has been applied; final alignment is left to you.

The cover's thin gold double border is printed ornament on the cover,
not a frame around the image. The upper-middle is blank for your HTML title.
The endpaper is opaque cream paper, with transparency outside its edges.
Alpha is real, not a painted checkerboard. Very faint antialiased texture
can remain near cutout edges; do not remove the alpha channel on import.

Resolution: the sizes above are final export dimensions. Native generation
was 1983 x 793 for each soil patch, 1774 x 887 for the sunflower sheet,
and 1122 x 1402 for the cover and endpaper. Exports use Lanczos resizing
with the alpha channel preserved. The sunflower sheet is an upscaled export,
not a native 4096 x 2048 generation.
