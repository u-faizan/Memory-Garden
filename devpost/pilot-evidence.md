## Motion performance and limits

The final unrecorded headless production run measured desktop median frame interval **16.7 ms**, p95 **33.3 ms**, maximum **149.8 ms**; mobile median **16.7 ms**, p95 **17.1 ms**, maximum **33.7 ms**. Desktop is not a proven constant-60-fps result. Exploratory fresh-browser runs were variable and included multi-second startup stalls; settled replay was smoother. Recording also adds overhead. Optional SVG turbulence and raster drop-shadow filters were removed to reduce rendering work, but their removal alone is not claimed to solve every cold-start stall. Assess the first click and replay in the learner's normal browser before accepting the motion.

Only Chromium was exercised here; real touch hardware, Safari and Firefox remain later responsive/accessibility checks. The garden source is native 1536×1024, not the proposed 2400×1600. The sunflower exceeds the proposed 150 KB target by 26,992 bytes; total art stays below 1.5 MB. Preserve detail for review, then agree any quality/compression adjustment. Early stages are reusable vectors; independently generated aligned growth paintings are not assumed.
