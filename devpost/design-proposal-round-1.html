A · The Secret Glade

Recommended direction

A close, enclosed clearing. Two curved beds embrace a small path; foreground leaves frame the viewer inside the garden. A physical journal with a cloth bookmark invites the first memory.

How it works as a place

  • Ten roots grouped within two continuous, gently shaped earth beds.
  • Small identity tag upper left; book/page controls upper right. No header or footer bands.
  • Sunflowers grow within the beds, with root shadow, depth scale and light foreground grass overlap.
  • “Plant a Memory” lives on the foreground journal bookmark, as one accessible button.
  • Mobile re-blocks the two curves and preserves the ten logical spots.

What choosing it means

Reuse the sunflower, illustration direction and useful growth behavior. Recompose the environment into a landscape and portrait pair, with sparse foreground and ground-contact artwork.

Refine the soil: the board's pronounced rings/deep holes are too strong. The intended plots are shallow marks in a continuous bed, with calmer edge detail so personal flowers remain the focus.

One continuous ground model keeps asset alignment and mobile layout simpler.

B · The Terraced Story Garden

Three gentle planting terraces and a short diagonal stair create a miniature landscape. Low stone edges separate front, middle and back, making the planting areas more architectural and explicit.

How it works as a place

  • Ten roots: four in front, three in the middle, three in back.
  • A small wooden identity tag and floating book/page controls sit inside the scene.
  • Back flowers are smaller; front flowers are larger. Stone and grass edges ground their stems.
  • “Plant a Memory” sits on a seed packet beside the path entrance.
  • Mobile stacks the terraces vertically, retaining all roots rather than cropping shelves away.

What choosing it means

Keep React/Vite/TypeScript, HTML controls and CSS/SVG motion. No 3D or game engine is needed.

A newly composed landscape/portrait pair needs aligned stone/grass occlusion layers. Depth sorting and touch spacing take more coordination than A.

Its arrangement is more formal. Keep stone edges and rows gentle so the space feels personal and peaceful.

The garden becomes a journal.

Desktop opens a two-page botanical book over the visible, softly dimmed garden. Mobile opens one comfortable page. These views share a journal identity; the functional controls will be real HTML.

The boards suggest atmosphere and proportions. Generated slot counts, dates, letterforms and exact controls are illustrative. Ten stable positions, readable contrast, 44 CSS px targets, real inputs and the requested four viewport sizes still need the chosen direction's layout proof. The final background will contain neither planted memory flowers nor functional text.

Recommendation: A · The Secret Glade

A's continuous ground, intimate enclosure and physically situated journal best support the personal sanctuary feeling with manageable layering. Soften its empty plots and reduce competing edge detail.

Choose B if the terraces better match the miniature-world feeling you want; the added art coordination is feasible within the selected stack.
